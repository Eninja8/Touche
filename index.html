First to

Applies from the next match.

  • One touch kills. A thrust's point is always lethal. So is any blade moving fast enough: flick it, dash with it, drop on someone.
  • Blades clash. Meet a thrust with your blade to parry it and stun the attacker for a free riposte.
  • Coup double. Touch each other at the same instant and nobody scores.
  • Move a lot. Jump twice, kick off walls, slide under a high blade while running, dash, or backstep by dashing with no direction held.
  • Esc pauses. M mutes.
